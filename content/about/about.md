## Who Are We?

We are the Music City Rust Developers. A collective of like minded developers who love learning about, teaching about and building solutions for 
business, hobby and or educational solutions using the [Rust Programming language](https://rust-lang.org/). We meet once a month at [Bitcoin Park](https://share.google/Lvs8lxYP0caX3Xsj8) 10 months out the year, weather permitting. We are not endorsed by any official Rust organization and are just a group of committed developers who share in a passion of building long term reliable software. 

## What is Rust?

Rust is a strongly typed programming langauge that places strong guard rails on what code is considered valid in order to create an easier to understand, easier to maintain and longer lasting software that can persist long into the future of compute technology. Rust is the langauge you want backing your legacy projects 20 years from now. 

## How Can I Learn More About Rust?

There are plenty of ways, you can read the documentation on the [home site](https://rust-lang.org/), [checkout the cookbook](https://rust-lang-nursery.github.io/rust-cookbook/) and go through excersizes with the [rustlings project](https://github.com/rust-lang/rustlings).
